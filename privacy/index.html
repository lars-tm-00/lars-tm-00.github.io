2. What we do

Larsomatic builds automation software for businesses (brand: Auftragspilot). Our apps connect to Meta platforms (Instagram, Facebook Pages, WhatsApp Business) so that business customers can receive, answer and organise messages and lead enquiries. We act as controller for our own accounts and as processor for data our business customers receive through their connected accounts.

3. Data we process

  • Account data: name, business name, email address and phone number of our business customers and contacts.
  • Data from Meta integrations (only after the account owner connected their account and only to the extent the permission was granted): Instagram-scoped user ID, username, profile name, content and timestamps of messages and comments sent to the connected account, WhatsApp phone number and profile name of the sender, content of lead-form answers.
  • Technical data: access tokens, webhook events, IP address and server logs.

4. Purposes and legal basis

  • Receiving and answering enquiries and messages for the connected business, and storing them in the customer's CRM (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Operating, securing and improving our service (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Following legal obligations such as bookkeeping (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not sell personal data and do not use data received from Meta for advertising, profiling or to train AI models.

5. Recipients and processors

We use service providers that process data on our behalf, for example hosting and automation (self-hosted n8n), CRM and database providers (e.g. Notion, Neon, Vercel), email providers (Google) and AI service providers for classifying and drafting replies. Where data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Message and lead data is deleted on request of the account owner or the person concerned, or when the business customer disconnects the account. Data we must keep by law (e.g. invoices) is kept for the statutory period.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Estonia: Andmekaitse Inspektsioon). To exercise your rights or to have your data deleted, see our data deletion instructions or write to lt@larsomatic.org.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this page.
